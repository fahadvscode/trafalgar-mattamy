A different Mattamy community, for context only

Mattamy Homes also markets Hawthorne East Village at Fourth Line and Louis St. Laurent Avenue in Milton, a currently-selling community of townhomes and detached homes starting from $903,990 with an estimated 2027 occupancy. That starting figure comes from a third-party listing, not from a Mattamy price sheet reproduced here, and it describes Hawthorne East Village only. Hawthorne on Trafalgar is an earlier-stage, not-yet-launched community on the opposite side of Milton, still moving through municipal approvals. The East Village number is not a forecast, a comparable sale, or a price for Trafalgar Road.
